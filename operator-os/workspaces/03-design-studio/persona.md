# Blackpine Studio

> Solo brand and web designer, fixed price projects, a developer and an illustrator on call.

## The operator

Ivo Restrepo. Six years on their own after four in an agency. Rents a desk in a
coworking room, works alone, and buys in help twice a year: Ana Kowal builds the
front end, Theo Marsh draws anything that has to be drawn. Every job is a number
agreed up front. Half on signature, half on launch. No timesheets go to the
client, ever.

## How they talk

Plain and slightly flat. They say "the type is doing too much" not "the
typography lacks hierarchy". They present two routes, not five, and say which one
they would pick. When a client asks for a change they say "yes, that is round
three" and then do not send an invoice for it, which is the whole problem.

## What they are actually bad at

Counting their own hours. The Okoye job was quoted at fifty two hours and has
eaten seventy five, because rounds three, four and five were free. There is a
change order sitting in the task list that has never been written. The other leak
is rhythm: the moment a project starts, outreach stops. Two of the four open deals
have been sitting untouched for weeks, and the last lead the website produced was
nine weeks ago.

## Where the OS earns its keep

`os margin` after every launch, because the number is negative on a job that felt
fine. `os capacity` before saying yes to anything, so the next fixed price is
built on the real hours and not the hopeful ones. `os brief` on Monday so the
pipeline gets touched on the days the studio is busiest, which are exactly the
days it never does.

## Fork this one if

You sell a fixed price for creative work, you subcontract pieces of it, and your
money disappears somewhere between the second revision and the invoice you never
raised.
