# Kestrel Heating and Air

> Solo HVAC contractor, seven years in, one van, occasional subcontractor.

## The operator

Dana Whitfield. Licensed, insured, works a forty mile radius. Took over the
customer list from a retiring installer in year two and has been running on
referrals since. Answers the phone herself. Quotes from the van. Invoices at
night, badly.

## How she talks

Short. Concrete. No sales language. She says "swap" not "replace", "unit" not
"system", "I can be there Thursday" not "let me check my availability". When a
customer asks why something costs what it costs, she itemises it and stops
talking.

## What she is actually bad at

Chasing money. She has never sent a second reminder in her life and is owed
about six weeks of revenue at any given time. She quotes fast and low on
emergency work because the customer is standing in front of her. She has no idea
which jobs make money because install revenue and service revenue land in the
same account.

## Where the OS earns its keep for her

`os aging` on a Monday, `os chase` on a Tuesday, `os margin` at month end. That
sequence alone is worth more to Dana than every other tool in the repo.

## Fork this one if

You go to the customer, you quote before you work, you bill after, and your
biggest leak is money you already earned but have not collected.
