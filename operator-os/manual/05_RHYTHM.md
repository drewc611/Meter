# 05 Rhythm

The tools are worth nothing without a cadence. This is the cadence. It totals
about ninety minutes a week.

## Every working day, five minutes

`day`. Read `os brief`. Pick three things. Say the one thing that must be true by
tonight. Stop.

## End of every working day, three minutes

`time`. Log what you worked on and for how long, including the hours nobody
pays for. This is the entry most people skip and the one that makes `pricing`
and `capacity` tell the truth later.

## Every week, one fixed morning, twenty minutes

In this order, because the order is the point.

1. `chase`. Money already earned. Highest return per minute in the system.
2. `pipeline`. Every open deal gets a next action with a date.
3. `followup`. Quotes with no answer, jobs with no review asked for, customers
   gone quiet.
4. `schedule`. Fit the week into the hours that exist. Name what is not getting
   done.

## Every Friday, twenty minutes

`week`. Six answers, written to a file. Money in, money owed, work, demand,
loops, one decision.

## Every month, forty minutes

1. `pricing`. What each job actually made. Find the pattern in the losers.
2. `taxset`. Move the money that is not yours.
3. `reaper`. Remove what is dead. Read every recurring cost out loud.
4. Update `manual/01_REGISTRY.md`.

## Every quarter, an hour

Read six weeks of `data/notes/weeks/`. Three questions:

- What did I say I would decide and then not decide?
- Which shape of work made money and which shape made noise?
- What is the constraint now, and is it the same one as last quarter?

## When the rhythm breaks

It will. The recovery is always the same and it is never "catch up on
everything": run `reaper`, then `chase`, then `day`. Dead rows out, money in,
three things. You are back inside ten minutes.
