# What you own

Plain writing, before you pay. The formal version is in `LICENSE` and says the
same things in the language a court reads. If the two ever disagree, that is a
mistake and this file is what was meant.

## Your data is yours, completely

Everything under `data/` belongs to you. It sits on your own disk in plain CSV
files. It is never uploaded, never synced, never seen by us, and there is no
account holding it. If you delete this repo tomorrow, copy `data/` first and you
still have your entire business.

We cannot lock you out of your own data because we were never holding it.

## What you can do with the software

You bought a permanent licence for one business, yours. With it you can:

- install it on every machine you personally use
- change any file in it, including the engine and the tools
- keep using this version forever, with no renewal and no subscription
- keep using it if we stop publishing updates, go quiet, or shut down
- write your own tools, workspaces and commands and keep them

## What you cannot do

- resell it, republish it, or give copies to other businesses
- sell it as your own product, with or without changes
- use it to run a service where other businesses pay you to operate it for them
- remove the licence file from copies you make for your own use

If you want to use it for clients, ask. There is a separate arrangement for that
and it is not expensive.

## What you get, exactly

- the full repository, every file, on day one
- the written workbook, nine modules, Mac and Windows
- thirty-five tools and forty three commands
- eight import adapters and the contract for writing your own
- the plugin SDK, with four working examples
- the agent layer: routing table, roster, and five scheduled runs
- eight encoded workspaces with personas
- the local console
- updates to this major version, for at least twelve months from purchase

## Plugins you write, and plugins other people write

Anything you build on top of this is yours. Your own plugins, adapters,
workspaces, tools and commands belong to you completely, and you may sell them,
give them away, or keep them private. That is the whole point of the capability
model existing.

What you may not do is repackage this product as the base of your own competing
product. Extend it, sell what you built, and leave the core alone.

Plugins written by other people are their software, not mine. A plugin runs as
code on your machine with the access its manifest declares, and nothing in this
licence makes me responsible for one I did not write. `os plugin verify` checks
a plugin against its own checksums and tells you what it declared. Read a plugin
before you install it, exactly as you would any other code.

## What is not included

No live sessions. No calls. No implementation service. This is a self serve
product on purpose, so nothing about it depends on anyone's calendar. If you want
someone to sit with you while you set it up, that is a separate paid thing and it
is not required.

## Refunds

If you have not got value from it, say so within thirty days and you get your
money back. You do not have to prove anything or send the files back. Keep them.
The reason this offer is safe to make is that the workbook has a finish line in
every module: if module 01 did not end with your own business printing on your
own screen, it did not work, and you should ask.

## Support

Email, and a reply within two working days. Support covers the product: installs,
errors, and how a tool is meant to work. It does not cover your accounting, your
pricing, or your customers, because those are yours.

## Warranty, honestly

This is software that reports arithmetic over files you control. It is not a bank,
an accountant, a lawyer, or a filing service, and it never touches money. Its
tax figures are a planning percentage you chose applied to cash you actually
received, and they are not a calculation of what you owe. Take them to whoever
does your return.

Check your own numbers. The tools are built so you can: every figure comes from a
row you can open, and every tool ends with something you verify yourself.

## If we sell the project or stop working on it

Your licence survives both. It is permanent and it is not conditional on us still
being here.
