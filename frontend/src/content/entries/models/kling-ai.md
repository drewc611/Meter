Chinese video-generation platform built by short-video company Kuaishou, reaching a stable Kling 3.0 release on February 7, 2026. Kuaishou has since spun Kling out as a separately funded subsidiary, with Alibaba and Tencent both joining a 2026 financing round at an $18B valuation while Kuaishou retained majority control.
