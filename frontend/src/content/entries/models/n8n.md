A workflow automation platform with native AI Agent and LLM nodes, letting teams wire AI models and business tools together visually rather than in code. Self-hostable under a fair-code license; the hosted cloud product bills per completed workflow run regardless of how many nodes or steps it contains, and separately meters credits for its own AI workflow-builder assistant.
