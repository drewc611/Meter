AWS's own foundation model family, positioned on cost-effectiveness rather than raw frontier capability. The current Nova 2 generation includes Nova 2 Lite (text/reasoning, 1M-token context) and Nova 2 Sonic (real-time speech-to-speech across seven languages), alongside a multimodal embeddings model, Nova Forge for custom model training, and Nova Act for browser-automation agents.
