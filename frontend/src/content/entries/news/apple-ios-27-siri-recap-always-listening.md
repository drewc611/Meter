Apple confirmed on September 9, 2026 that iOS 27 ships as a free update on Monday, September 14 -- headlined by a rebuilt Siri, co-developed using Google's Gemini models, with its own dedicated app, a mix of on-device and Private Cloud Compute processing, and a Dynamic Island launcher. Apple's own framing: "iOS 27 will be available as a free software update on Monday, September 14."

## The feature that raises the real question

A companion feature, Siri Recap, requires an Apple Watch Series 12 or Ultra 4 paired with an iPhone 16 or later. It passively listens through the day and generates text summaries via Secure Enclave processing -- no audio is stored, and summaries auto-delete after seven days unless a user saves them. Apple's own description of the boundary: "Siri Recap doesn't produce a full transcript of a conversation. It's just a summary." The feature omits financial data, government IDs, and speaker identification by design.

## A live case study for corporate AI policy

An always-listening feature shipping from a major consumer device maker, with specific, disclosed limits on what it stores and for how long, is a real-world reference point for any company writing its own policy on AI note-takers and meeting assistants -- the data-retention tradeoffs Apple is disclosing here (what's captured, what's stored, what auto-deletes) are the exact questions enterprise privacy and security teams are already asking about similar tools in the workplace.
