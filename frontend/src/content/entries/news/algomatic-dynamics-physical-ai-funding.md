Algomatic Dynamics, a Tokyo-based robotics startup led by CEO Yuki Nanri, raised ¥5 billion (roughly $32.5 million) from DMM.com in its first dedicated financing round, announced September 9, 2026. The company split off from DMM Group's AI venture Algomatic in a June 2026 restructuring, and is building AI-powered multi-fingered robotic hands, motion-data collection infrastructure, tactile sensing, and bipedal-locomotion control -- with a robotic-hand platform targeted for release in Japan by the end of 2026.

## Data infrastructure, not just the hardware

The company's own framing emphasizes reusability: "the company said the data can be used across different robot forms" -- positioning its motion-capture and tactile-sensing infrastructure as a data asset that outlives any single robot design, rather than a one-off engineering effort tied to one product.

## Part of a larger capital rotation

Crunchbase separately tracks robotics funding topping $18.8 billion for 2026 -- a $32.5 million round is modest against that backdrop, but it's a concrete, dated data point in a broader pattern of capital rotating from pure language-model infrastructure toward embodied and physical AI. For finance leaders tracking where AI capex goes next, funding announcements at this scale, across multiple geographies, are the early signal before the mega-rounds arrive.
