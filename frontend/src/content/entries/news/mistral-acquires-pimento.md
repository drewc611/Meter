Mistral AI confirmed on September 23 that it has acquired Pimento, a Paris startup whose software turns campaign briefs, brand identities, and campaign data into ad visuals and short social-media animations. It's Mistral's third acquisition of 2026. Pimento was founded in October 2022 by Florent Facq and Tomás Yany, and raised a €3 million seed round in December 2023 led by Partech and Cygni Capital.

## The price is genuinely unsettled

Mistral told AFP only that the price was "several million euros," without confirming an exact figure -- and the reporting that has filled the gap doesn't agree. French outlet L'Informé put the deal at around €3.8 million; Sifted, citing filings, reported €12.7 million in cash and shares. Both figures are small relative to Mistral's own scale after its €3 billion raise, but the size of the gap between them is worth flagging rather than picking one and presenting it as settled.

## It's an acquihire, not a product buy

Mistral says Pimento's staff are joining its engineering and product teams to work on Vibe, Mistral's conversational assistant (formerly Le Chat) -- not to keep running Pimento's ad-creative product as a separate line. That's consistent with the acquisition being priced more like a team buy than a revenue-multiple product acquisition, and it's the third such move by Mistral this year, a pattern of building out consumer-product capability by acquiring small teams rather than hiring them individually.
