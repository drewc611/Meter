Meta has acquired Stilla, an AI agent startup founded in Stockholm in 2024, Axios reported September 9, 2026. No deal price was disclosed, and neither Meta nor Stilla responded to requests for comment. Stilla emerged from stealth in January 2026 with just $5 million in pre-seed funding, founded by former Shopify executives Siavash Ghorbani and Kaj Drobin.

## A small, fast acqui-hire for a specific product gap

Stilla built AI agents that integrate with workplace tools like Slack, Linear, GitHub, and Notion to maintain shared context and permissions across a team's tools and workflows -- infrastructure-layer work rather than a consumer-facing product. Meta is folding the team and technology into Business Agent, its AI system for handling merchant conversations and transactions across WhatsApp, Messenger, and Instagram, which the company says is already used by more than a million businesses. An eight-month-old startup with a single small funding round getting bought rather than continuing to raise is the acqui-hire pattern playing out across enterprise AI tooling this year, where the buyer wants a working team and a specific technical capability more than an independent business.

## What it signals about Meta's messaging AI push

The deal underscores Meta's push to bring its AI technology to the commercial foundation of its messaging apps, rather than treating chatbots as a support-cost feature bolted onto WhatsApp and Instagram. Buying a permissions-and-context layer for AI agents, instead of building it internally, suggests Meta sees that as a solved problem worth acquiring rather than a core differentiator worth building from scratch -- a build-vs-buy signal other platforms racing to ship agentic commerce features are likely watching too.
