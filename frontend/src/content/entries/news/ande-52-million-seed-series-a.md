Ande raised $52 million in combined seed and Series A funding from Lightspeed Venture Partners, Redpoint Ventures, Duration Ventures, and Sierra Ventures, with Bain Capital Ventures also participating. The company is pitching itself as an "entertainment operating system" for enterprises -- an agentic platform that automates the client dinners, sporting events, and corporate experiences that Ande says enterprises spend an estimated $325 billion on annually.

## What the agent actually does

Ande's workflow covers venue availability, booking, approval routing, contract execution, and expense reconciliation -- the full loop from "we want to take this client somewhere" to a closed-out expense report, without a human doing the manual coordination at each step. It's a narrower, more operational use of agentic AI than most funding headlines this category produces: not a chatbot layered onto an existing tool, but automation of a specific, recurring back-office workflow that was previously handled by admins and expense systems bolted together.

## Traction claims

Ande says more than 60 enterprises use the platform, including Cloudflare, Salesforce, and McGraw Hill, with customers reporting 12-15% savings and more than $400 million in entertainment spend now flowing through the platform annually. As with any early-stage funding announcement, these are the company's own disclosed numbers, not independently audited -- worth taking as a directional claim about traction rather than an audited figure.
